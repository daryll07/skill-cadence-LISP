--- Trace Width Formulas ---
IPC-D-275:
  External: W = ((I / (0.0647 * T^0.4281))^(1/0.6732)) / (oz * 1.378)
  Internal: W = ((I / (0.0150 * T^0.5453))^(1/0.7349)) / (oz * 1.378)

IPC-2221:
  Area = (I / (k * T^0.44))^(1/0.725)
  W = Area / (oz * 1.378)
  (External k=0.048 | Internal k=0.024)

IPC-2152 (Conservative Approx):
  k=0.0349 using IPC-2221 base equation structure.

--- Via Current & Advanced Math Summaries ---
• Ampacity: Uses the IPC-2221 thermal curve-fit baseline.
• Exact Geometric Area: Uses the true cross-sectional area of a hollow cylinder [ A = π * (D - t) * t ] for highest accuracy.
• Electrical Math: Calculates DC resistance using copper resistivity (1.724e-6 Ω·cm) and applies the copper temperature coefficient (0.00393) to calculate worst-case hot resistance.
